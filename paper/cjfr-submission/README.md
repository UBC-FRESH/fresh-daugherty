# CJFR submission package

For Canadian Journal of Forest Research (single-blind review).

- `manuscript.pdf` — the complete manuscript (title, author, abstract, text,
  figure, references, and CJFR declarations: Acknowledgements, Author
  contributions, Competing interests, Data availability). Single-blind: author
  identity and the public UBC-FRESH repository are referenced openly.
- `title-page.pdf` — the title page (title, author, affiliation, corresponding
  author, keywords, article type).
- `fig1_declining_ndy.pdf` — Figure 1 (also embedded in the manuscript), for
  separate upload at resolution.

A cover letter is written at submission time. The benchmark record
(`results/experiments/grid.csv` + `grid_trajectories.csv`) is tracked in the
repository; a DOI-archived release (Zenodo) will be cited at submission.
